# Gujarati / Hindi reel observations — 19 October 2026

Research used normal, user-authorized Instagram browsing. No account credentials,
private collections, screenshots of private content, or creator media are bundled
in the application. Public audio IDs and public reference reels are the evidence.
Observations are a dated sample, not a live or comprehensive trend leaderboard.

## Coverage and confidence

Five public audio pages were inspected, with the first twelve visible reel tiles
per page. Four individual reels were visually inspected (Hindi festive couple,
Hindi fairground memory, Surat Garba detail/recap and a second Gujarati creator’s festival edit), a second current Garba reel's
metadata was checked, and one creator's first twelve non-pinned reel counts were
read for a baseline. Tile images are not a substitute for watching every reel.
One baseline supports an account-specific outlier; it does not establish which
format over-indexes across the whole platform. Likes, views and audio use counts
are kept separate. Rounded usage totals do not establish growth, trend velocity,
or a causal effect of a song on reach. No viral-view guarantee is offered.

## Original implementation

Six new editable arrangements adapt general mechanics: opening hero image,
short details, a reveal or group finale, and slower memory holds. Their titles,
timing, original instrumental and sample images are independently authored.
The tempo is an editable design choice, **not a measured BPM or beat map of the
linked song**. Generated examples use the project's existing licensed stock photo
inputs and original synth music. No creator video, voice, song recording,
watermark or distinctive layout was downloaded or reproduced.

Each arrangement has an Instagram song guide with the observed title/artist,
audio-page link, reference link, date and evidence label. “Build for Instagram
song” produces a montage with no music; export it and choose the linked audio
inside Instagram. The existing Creative → Audio upload remains available for
music the user is licensed to include in exported files.

[Instagram's licensed music help](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/402084904469945)
explains that library access depends on account/post type. Instagram availability
is not permission to bundle a song recording in a third-party application's
public samples. No recording or availability is promised outside Instagram.
